How large Magic events have worked

Three historical lanes — we need Wizards to place us in one.

1. Official Premier Play

World Championship, Pro Tour, Spotlight Series, Regional Championships / RCQs — run by Wizards or select organizers under Wizards systems (EventLink, MTR, invitation policy).

2. Exclusive large-event partners

2018–2020 Grand Prix / MagicFest era: Wizards appointed ChannelFireball Events as exclusive global GP partner to standardize quality — proving large events are a controlled partnership, not open DIY.

3. Independent series with coordination

Historic SCG Opens and GPs were large open fields with direct entry fees — the closest precedent to our model — run with tournament-rules culture, clear non-affiliation (when independent), and calendar coordination so they complemented OP.

What “approval” typically covers

Historically, large-event greenlights are about control — not just a prize check.

  • Written IP / trademark license — permission to use Magic names, logos, and official marks beyond Fan Content / WPN marketing limits.
  • Prize & entry exceptions — waiver of community caps ($5K participant payments / $25 entry) via tournaments@wizards.com pathway.
  • Sanctioning decision — rated/Premier vs. unsanctioned-but-authorized; EventLink reporting; invitation / PQ implications if any.
  • Broadcast & media rights — live stream, VOD, and any TV/other medium permissions beyond free community streaming rules.
  • Sponsor category approval — Wizards retains veto on partners (guidelines already ban certain categories).
  • Brand confusion controls — naming, trade dress, and “official / presented by Wizards” claims tightly defined.
  • Integrity & conduct — judges, MTR/IPG adherence, suspended-player handling, Code of Conduct.

Brand protection

Magic’s reputation is the product. We treat it that way.

  • No false official claims — marketing reviewed against Fan Content + Marketing Materials Policy until a license says otherwise.
  • No prohibited sponsors — alcohol, tobacco, drugs, weapons, adult content, rival game companies, and any category Wizards adds.
  • Wizards veto — written right to reject sponsors, naming, and keystone creative.
  • Universes Beyond respect — no unauthorized mashups or partner-IP misuse.
  • Family-friendly floor — venue, alcohol, and age policies aligned with current WPN expectations and local law.

Organized Play alignment

Complement Premier Play — never confuse it.

Lesson from the GP exclusivity era and from independent Opens: players need calendar clarity and brand clarity. We will schedule and name the event only with Wizards’ guidance.

We will not

Style an open buy-in as a Pro Tour, World Championship, or MagicFest; combine third-party prizes into a Premier Event in a confusing way; or open registration / buy-ins before written authorization.

We will

Share draft calendar against Regional Championships / Spotlight Series; accept “no” on conflicting weekends; and use naming that Wizards Legal approves.

Organizer readiness (draft)

What we will put in place before any public announcement.

  • Legal entity & insurance — event liability, prize surety/escrow, and indemnification language mirroring Wizards’ community terms.
  • Venue shortlist — convention-capable sites with security, accessibility, and tabletop capacity.
  • Judge & TO pipeline — experienced championship staff; no dual TO/player conflicts per MTR.
  • Broadcast plan — free public stream (no paywall); TV/other mediums only if Wizards permits.
  • Retail / WPN respect — local store involvement options so the event supports, not cannibalizes, hobby channel health.

Credentials and partner names can be supplied under NDA in a follow-up packet once Wizards indicates interest in a deeper review.
