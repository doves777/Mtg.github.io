The market

Magic is a global entertainment franchise — not a niche hobby.

Sponsors get access to a passionate, high-intent audience that already spends on digital platforms, live events, collectibles, and lifestyle brands.

Why now

The gap: prestige prize money without a brand-native platform.

Official World Championship events have reached ~$1M purses — then contracted. Players, creators, and fans still want a marquee destination. Brands want ownership of the story, not a logo in the corner of someone else’s broadcast.

Proven ceiling

Magic World Championship XXVI/XXVII posted $1.0M–$1.05M prize pools — proof the category can carry seven-figure stakes.

Open lane

Independent organizers can package title rights, hospitality, creator talent, and retail activations that official circuits rarely sell as a bundle.

Culture moment

Universes Beyond crossovers and record set interest have pulled new audiences into Magic — ideal timing for mainstream brand entry.

Audience

Who shows up — and who watches.

Magic’s core is strategic, adult, and brand-receptive: collectors, digital natives, and live-event travelers who follow creators as closely as they follow the metagame.

Player profile

Average tabletop player ~30; majority aged 13–45. Mix of lifelong fans and recent converts (roughly equal thirds under 3 / up to 10 / 10+ years).

Sponsor-fit verticals

Consumer tech, financial services, energy & lifestyle beverages, peripherals, travel, telecom, and premium consumer brands seeking cultural credibility with 18–44 audiences.

Engagement style

High dwell-time streams, Discord communities, and local game-store networks that amplify partner offers beyond the weekend itself.

Production

Designed like a championship, not a local FNM.

  • Broadcast-first stage — feature matches, player cams, analyst desk, and highlight packages built for Twitch/YouTube clips.
  • Creator lane — invite watch-party talent and co-streamers with sponsor-safe integration windows.
  • Fan floor — side events, meet-and-greets, retail demo pods, and partner experiential builds.
  • Press & prestige — media day, champion crowning moment, and season-long content recap series.
