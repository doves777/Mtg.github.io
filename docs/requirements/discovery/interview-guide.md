# Customer Discovery — Interview Guide

A comprehensive question bank for vendor interviews. **You will not ask every question** — pick the sections that match the vendor and follow the pain. Each section is tagged to the requirement areas it informs so findings feed straight back into the [requirements docs](../README.md).

> Tip: lead with story-based prompts ("walk me through…"), then use the follow-up probes to get specifics, numbers, and emotion. Save any product talk for the very end (see [Wrap-Up](#14-wrap-up--pitch-last)).

**Legend of tags:** `INV` Inventory · `PRC` Pricing · `ORD` Orders · `POS` Checkout · `EVT` Events · `CX` Customer · `BUY` Buying/Buylist

---

## 1. Warm-Up & Business Profile

*Set context and build rapport. Establishes how to weight their answers.*

- Tell me about your business — what do you sell and where?
- What mix of products do you carry? (sports cards, Pokémon, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece, Lorcana, other TCGs, sealed, supplies)
- How long have you been vending at shows?
- How many shows do you do in a typical month or year? What size are they?
- Do you also sell online, in a storefront, or only at shows?
- How big is your inventory, roughly? (number of cards / SKUs)
- How many people work a booth with you at a typical show?
- What does a great show day look like vs. a bad one?

---

## 2. A Typical Show Day (Story)  ·  `EVT` `POS` `ORD`

*The single most valuable section. Get the end-to-end narrative before drilling in.*

- Walk me through your **last** show from setup to teardown.
- What do you do the night before / morning of to get ready?
- When it gets busy, where do things break down or slow down?
- What's the most frustrating part of a show day?
- If you could wave a wand and fix one thing about show days, what would it be?

**Probes:** How long did setup take? How many customers at peak? Where was the line longest? What went wrong last time?

---

## 3. Inventory Management  ·  `INV`

- How do you keep track of what inventory you have?
- How do you decide what to bring to a given show? *(also `EVT`)*
- How is inventory organized at the booth? (binders, showcases, boxes, bins)
- How do you know when something has sold — is your count ever wrong?
- How do you handle graded cards vs. raw/bulk?
- How do you add new inventory into your system? How long does that take?
- What details do you track per card? (condition, set, variant, grade, language, cost)
- What happens to your counts across multiple shows or channels (online + shows)?

**Probes:** What tool holds the truth today? Last time your count was wrong, what happened? How many hours/week on inventory entry?

---

## 4. Price Lookup & Pricing  ·  `PRC`

- When a customer asks "how much for this card," what do you (or your staff) do?
- How long does a price lookup take when it's busy?
- What sources do you check for prices? (TCGplayer, eBay sold, 130point, PSA, apps…)
- How do you keep prices consistent across employees and across booths?
- How often do prices feel wrong or out of date?
- How do you price by condition or grade?
- Do you price differently for shows vs. online vs. store vs. wholesale?
- How do you handle discounts and haggling? Who's allowed to give them?
- How do you update prices in bulk when the market moves?

**Probes:** Show me the last card you looked up — how'd you do it? Ever lose a sale because pricing took too long? Ever undercharge/overcharge due to a lookup mistake?

---

## 5. Checkout & Payments  ·  `POS` `ORD`

- How do you build up a customer's order and total it?
- What do you use to take payment today? (Square, Clover, PayPal, cash, Venmo…)
- How long does a typical checkout take? What makes it slow?
- How do you handle a customer buying many cards at once?
- Do you do partial payments, holds, or deposits?
- How do you handle refunds or returns?
- Do you give/print receipts? Do customers ask for them?
- What device(s) do you run checkout on? (phone, tablet, laptop)

**Probes:** Walk me through the last checkout that felt painful. How often do totals get added up by hand? Ever have a payment/checkout failure at a show?

---

## 6. Connectivity & Offline  ·  `PRC` `POS`

*This is a hypothesized differentiator — validate whether the pain is real.*

- How's the internet/Wi-Fi/cell signal at the shows you attend?
- What happens to your workflow when the connection drops?
- Have you ever been unable to look up a price or take payment because you were offline?
- What do you do as a backup when that happens?
- How often does connectivity actually cause problems? (every show / sometimes / rarely)
- Does poor signal ever cost you sales or slow the line?

**Probes:** Tell me about the last time connectivity bit you. What's your fallback — paper, calculator, memory? How much do you think it cost you?

---

## 7. Online Preorders & Show Pickup  ·  `ORD` `CX` `EVT`

*The other hypothesized differentiator.*

- Do customers ever ask to buy before a show and pick up at your booth? How do you handle it now?
- Do you post inventory anywhere ahead of a show? (Instagram, Facebook groups, Discord, webstore)
- How do you take and track a preorder or hold today?
- How do you reserve the item so it isn't double-sold?
- How do customers know where/when to pick up?
- What goes wrong with preorders/holds today?
- How much of your show revenue could come from preorders if it were easy?

**Probes:** Walk me through the last preorder/hold you did. Ever double-sell a held card? How do you communicate pickup details today?

---

## 8. Buying, Trade-In & Buylist  ·  `BUY`

- Do you buy cards from customers — at shows, in-store, or online?
- Walk me through the last time you bought a collection or singles from someone.
- How do you decide what to offer? What sources/rules do you use?
- How long does it take to evaluate and quote a buy?
- Do employees make offers, or do you approve them? Where's the risk?
- Do you offer cash vs. store credit vs. trade differently?
- How do bought cards get into your inventory afterward?
- Do customers submit lists of what they want to sell ahead of time?
- What's the hardest part of buying — pricing, speed, trust, condition disputes?

**Probes:** How much of your acquisition happens at shows? Ever overpay/underpay and regret it? How long from "buy" to "listed for sale"?

---

## 9. Staff, Roles & Permissions  ·  `POS` `PRC` `BUY`

- Who works your booth, and are they permanent or temporary/seasonal?
- How do you train new or temporary staff? How long until they're useful?
- What are employees allowed to do without you? (discounts, price overrides, buys)
- How do you keep an eye on what staff are doing during a show?
- Have you had pricing mistakes, unauthorized discounts, or shrinkage from staff?

**Probes:** How long to onboard a temp? What decisions require you specifically? Any incident that made you tighten controls?

---

## 10. Reporting & Decisions  ·  `EVT` `INV`

- After a show, how do you figure out what sold and how you did?
- How do you decide what to bring to the next, similar show?
- What numbers do you actually care about? (revenue, margin, top sellers, dead stock)
- How long after a show until you know your results?
- What would you love to know that you can't easily see today?

**Probes:** Show me how you tracked your last show's results. What decision would better data change?

---

## 11. Current Tools & Switching  ·  all areas

- What software/tools do you use today across inventory, pricing, checkout, and buying? *(Note stack; overlap with [competitive review](../13-roadmap-and-next-steps.md#6-build-a-competitive-review))*
- What do you like about your current setup? What do you dislike?
- Have you tried other card-vendor tools? Why did you stop or keep going?
- What would it take for you to switch tools?
- What has stopped you from switching before? (setup effort, data migration, cost, habit)
- How painful was setup/onboarding for your current tools?

**Probes:** Which tool would be hardest to give up and why? What's your dealbreaker in new software?

---

## 12. Value & Willingness to Pay

*Approach carefully — anchor on current spend and pain, not a hypothetical price.*

- Roughly what do you spend today on the tools/services we discussed?
- How do you think about the cost of the problems we talked about (lost sales, time, errors)?
- If a tool reliably fixed [their #1 stated pain], what would that be worth to you?
- How do you prefer to pay for software? (monthly, per show, per seat, % of sales)
- Who decides on new tools for your business?

**Probes:** What would make this a no-brainer purchase vs. a maybe? What price would feel too expensive / suspiciously cheap?

---

## 13. Buyer Behavior (Vendor's View)  ·  `CX`

- What do your customers complain about most at your booth?
- Do customers ask to see your inventory before a show?
- Do repeat customers tell you what they're hunting for (want lists)?
- What would make your customers' experience noticeably better?

**Probes:** What's the most common customer request you *can't* easily fulfill today?

---

## 14. Wrap-Up & Pitch (Last)

*Only now, if it fits, share the concept and gauge reaction honestly.*

- Of everything we discussed, what's the single biggest headache?
- If I built something to fix that, would you want to hear about it? *(a real "yes" = a follow-up commitment: a call, a pilot, an intro)*
- **[Optional, brief]** Here's what we're exploring: faster show inventory + order building, offline pricing, and letting customers preorder for booth pickup. What's your gut reaction?
- Who else should I talk to? Can you introduce me? *(referrals = discovery gold)*
- Can I follow up as we make progress?

---

## Quick-Reference: Top 12 If You Only Have 15 Minutes

1. Walk me through your last show, setup to teardown. `EVT`
2. When it's busy, where does it break down? `POS`
3. How do you look up a price when it's busy, and how long does it take? `PRC`
4. How do you keep prices consistent across staff? `PRC`
5. How do you total an order and take payment today? `POS`
6. What happens when the Wi-Fi/signal drops? `PRC` `POS`
7. How do you track what inventory you have and what sold? `INV`
8. How do you decide what to bring to a show? `EVT` `INV`
9. Do customers ask to preorder/hold for pickup? How do you handle it? `ORD` `CX`
10. Do you buy from customers, and how do you price/approve it? `BUY`
11. What tools do you use now, and what would make you switch? *(all)*
12. What's your single biggest headache — and who else should I talk to?
