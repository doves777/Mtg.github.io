# Customer Discovery — Screener

Use this to qualify vendors **before** booking a full interview, so your 10–20 sessions hit the right profile (see [target customers](../00-product-overview.md#target-customers)). Keep it to ~2 minutes — a form or DM works.

## Who we want (target profile)

- Medium-to-large trading card vendors who **sell at shows/conventions/events**.
- Multiple people help at the booth (or high enough volume that speed matters).
- Manage a **large inventory** and need quick lookup/checkout.
- Bonus: already experiment with preorders/pickup or buy from customers.

## Screener questions

1. Do you currently sell at card shows, conventions, or live events? *(Must be **yes**.)*
2. About how many shows do you do per year? *(Prefer regular vendors, e.g. 6+.)*
3. Roughly how large is your inventory? *(Prefer larger catalogs / many SKUs.)*
4. How many people typically work your booth? *(1 is fine; 2+ is ideal for staff/permission themes.)*
5. What do you primarily sell? (sports, Pokémon, Magic, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece, Lorcana, other TCG, sealed, mixed)
6. Do you also sell online or run a storefront? (online only / storefront / shows only / mix)
7. What tools do you use today for inventory, pricing, or checkout? *(Any answer is useful.)*
8. Do you ever buy cards from customers or take trade-ins? (yes / no)
9. Are you open to a 30–45 minute conversation about how you run shows? *(No selling — just learning.)*

## Prioritization

| Signal | Prioritize |
| --- | --- |
| Does many shows/year | ✅ Higher |
| Large inventory + multiple staff | ✅ Higher |
| Already tries preorders/pickup or buylist | ✅ Higher (informs differentiators) |
| One-off/hobby seller, tiny inventory | ⬇️ Lower (outside primary target) |

## Aim for a balanced sample

Mix sports-card vs TCG-focused, single- vs multi-booth, owners vs the staff who run checkout, and frequent- vs occasional-show vendors — so findings generalize instead of reflecting one niche.

## Recruiting channels (ideas)

- Card show promoter/organizer contacts and vendor lists.
- Vendor-heavy Facebook groups, Discords, and subreddits.
- In-person at shows (schedule a follow-up, don't interview mid-rush).
- Referrals from every interview — always ask "who else should I talk to?"

## Logistics checklist

- [ ] Confirm profile fits target before booking.
- [ ] Book 30–45 min; note their timezone.
- [ ] Ask permission to record/take notes.
- [ ] Have a copy of [`interview-notes-template.md`](./interview-notes-template.md) ready.
- [ ] Prepare a small thank-you (gift card / show credit) if you're offering one.
