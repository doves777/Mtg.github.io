# 00 · Product Overview

## Product Vision

Create a modern SaaS platform for medium-sized and larger trading card vendors who sell at card shows, conventions, and events. The platform helps vendors manage inventory, pricing, customer orders, show pickup orders, and point-of-sale workflows more efficiently — especially where Wi-Fi may be unreliable.

The product reduces manual lookup, pricing, order-tracking, and checkout friction while giving vendors better visibility into inventory and demand.

## Problem Statement

Many card show vendors still rely on manual processes during events:

- Employees manually look up card prices.
- Orders are added together by hand or through generic tools.
- Inventory may not be updated in real time.
- Pricing may be inconsistent across employees or booths.
- Wi-Fi at card shows can be unreliable.
- Online preorder or pickup workflows are often clunky or nonexistent.
- Existing card vendor software may feel outdated, under-iterated, or poorly optimized for show-day operations.

The result: slower checkout, more pricing errors, weaker inventory control, and a worse customer experience.

## Target Customers

### Primary

Medium-sized and larger trading card vendors who:

- Sell at card shows, conventions, and live events.
- Have multiple employees helping customers.
- Manage large card inventories.
- Need quick price lookup and checkout.
- Want to accept online orders for event pickup.
- Need pricing consistency across staff.

### Secondary (possible)

- Local card shops that also vend at shows.
- Online card sellers who do in-person events.
- Breakers or dealers with event inventory.
- Multi-location collectible businesses.

## User Types

| User Type | Needs |
| --- | --- |
| **Vendor Owner / Admin** | Manage inventory, pricing, employees, show setup, reporting, and orders. |
| **Employee / Sales Associate** | Quickly look up cards, build carts, quote customers, and complete orders. |
| **Customer / Buyer** | Browse available inventory, place online pickup orders, view prices, and complete purchases. |
| **Event / Show Manager Mode** *(future)* | Optional user type if the platform expands into show-specific tools or marketplace-style event pickup. |
